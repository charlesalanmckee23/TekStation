How the numbers work

  • Ascension levels do not stack on the same boss. Gamma grants +5, Beta +10, Alpha +15 — clearing all three on one boss still nets 15, not 30. Only your highest tier counts.
  • Tekgrams do stack, downward. An Alpha kill grants that boss's Gamma and Beta unlocks too, so you can skip straight to Alpha if you can field the army.
  • Guardian bosses give zero levels. Broodmother, Megapithecus, Dragon, the three Titans and Nunatak are Tek and Element only. Astraeos's older encounters — Thodes, Natrix, Hydraskos, Thanatos and its Manticore — stay optional and pay nothing. Levels come from the six canonical ascensions plus the Astraeos Kraken.
  • The Kraken pays once, for the pair. The Heaven & Abyss update added two ascension fights to Astraeos: Abyssalus below the waves and Shallocis on the surface. Beating both on Alpha grants +15 one time, not +15 each — that single ascension is what lifted the ceiling from 230 to 245.
  • King Titan can't be rushed. Each tier requires the previous tier's trophy, so it's a forced Gamma → Beta → Alpha run. Alpha still pays the full 15 without watching the earlier cutscenes, but you forfeit the 5 and 10 from Gamma and Beta if you skip them.
  • Explorer Notes give levels, never Tekgrams. Same for Bob's Tall Tales notes and Chibi-Pets. No collectible in the game unlocks Tek.
  • Amber "verify" badges mark encounters where the public engram list is incomplete or newer than the wiki — mostly Ragnarok, Lost Colony and Genesis. Level totals for those are excluded rather than guessed.
  • Boss and item artwork is loaded from the community wikis, with original line-art glyphs underneath as a fallback. Offline, or if an image is missing, the glyph shows instead and nothing breaks — use Artwork: off to force glyphs everywhere.
  • Level ceiling assumes the standard ASA ruleset. Unofficial servers using OverrideMaxExperiencePointsPlayer or engram overrides will differ.
